Dr. George L Cowan, MD

Dr. Cowan is an independent psychiatrist at the Counseling and Psychiatry on Dargan Clinic (CAPD) in Florence, South Carolina.

Dr. Cowan serves as the Medical Director for The Owls Nest Recovery Community in Florence, SC. He assists patients in the medical management of their psychiatric medications and treats adults (18yo+).

For appointments or inquiries into Dr. Cowan’s practice, please call 843-503-1235.

Location

Located at 523 South Dargan Street in the historic district of Florence, South Carolina, CAP on Dargan is a whole-mind clinic. Patients can find independent psychiatrists, counselors, psychologists and support staff to help facilitate the best care possible.
