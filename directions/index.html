Directions

From US-52 North

Follow US-52 into Florence and turn left onto Cedar Street. Travel one block and the office will be in the corner of Cedar and Dargan Streets.

From Myrtle Beach

Follow US-501 N, SC-576, and US-76 into Florence. Turn left onto S Dargan Street, stay straight through the light at Pine Street, and continue two blocks. The office will be on your right.

From US-52 South

Follow US-52 into Florence and turn right onto Cedar Street. Travel one block and the office will be in the corner of Cedar and Dargan Streets.

From I-20

Keep left to continue on I-20BSE. Follow David McLeod Blvd (signs for US-76). Use the left two lanes to turn left onto US-76. Continue for 2.5 miles and turn right onto S Dargan Street. Stay straight through the light at Pine Street and continue two blocks. The office will be on your right.

Location

Located at 523 South Dargan Street in the historic district of Florence, South Carolina, CAP on Dargan is a whole-mind clinic. Patients can find independent psychiatrists, counselors, psychologists and support staff to help facilitate the best care possible.
