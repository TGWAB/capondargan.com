Comprehensive Mental Health Treatment

CAP on Dargan is a collaboration of independent psychiatry and psychology providers under one roof to help our patients receive the best and most well-rounded care with ease.

Emergency Services are not available at this time. If you are in a crisis, please call 911 and get to the nearest Emergency Room for critical care. If you are in a crisis but do not need acute, medical attention, we strongly recommend contacting The Carolina Center for Behavioral Health 24/7 Crisis Line by calling 864-235-2335.

Location

Located at 523 South Dargan Street in the historic district of Florence, South Carolina, CAP on Dargan is a whole-mind clinic. Patients can find independent psychiatrists, counselors, psychologists and support staff to help facilitate the best care possible.
